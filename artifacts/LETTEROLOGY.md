## The operation

We **letterize usernames**, not birth names.

1. Strip furniture (`@`, case, accents fold, `._-` are token breaks).
2. First letter sits the **house** (role).
3. Weight the rest (repeats + edges). Heaviest two after the signature are **manner** (how) and **field** (where).
4. Digits are the **Count**: 1=A … 9=I, 0=F (Fool). They visit unless the handle has almost no letters.
5. Offices recover Sisaveli's tiers as ranks *inside a name*, not a caste of the alphabet: Supreme / Strong / Ordinary / Gentle / Silent.
